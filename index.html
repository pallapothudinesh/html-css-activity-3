Behind the Neural Engine

Deep Tech Architecture

How our multi-cluster neural supercomputers power millions of concurrent inference streams.

01

Distributed Flash-Attention & Tensor Sharding

Our custom hardware-aware kernels distribute attention matrices across thousands of interconnected H100/B200 GPU clusters via 3.2 Tbps NVLink interconnects, preventing memory fragmentation.

02

Dynamic Sparse Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)

A learned top-2 gating router directs incoming tokens to designated specialized sub-networks, ensuring maximum capacity utilization with minimal floating-point energy overhead.

03

Recursive Self-Supervised Alignment (RLAIF)

Constitutional alignment training where adversarial critic agents continuously critique, score, and sanitize generation outputs for strict factuality and ethical constraints.

04

Speculative Token Streaming Pipeline

Small lightweight draft models pre-generate prospective token sequences while the full foundation model verifies them in parallel, boosting output token rates by up to 300%.
